# Code of Conduct

I want this project to be a welcoming place to learn. Everyone taking part - maintainers,
contributors and users - is expected to follow these standards.

## standards

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be inclusive: no harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sexualised language or
  imagery, whatever someone's background or experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, especially in code review.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's personal information without permission.
- Keep discussion on topic and professional.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do
not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behaviour they judge inappropriate. Report problems to the maintainers through a private channel
offered by the hosting platform (for example, GitHub's moderation and reporting tools). Reports
will be handled confidentially where possible.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
